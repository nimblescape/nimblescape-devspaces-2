Where the codicons are used, and what was changed:
- resources/icon.svg (the icon of Devspaces in the activity bar): the codicon "open-in-window",
  not changed.
- resources/icon.png (the icon of the extension, for example on the Marketplace): the codicon
  "open-in-window" in white on a blue square with round corners, 256 x 256 pixels. Changed: color,
  size and background.
- resources/icons/devspace-icons.woff (the icons of the rows of the view): shapes of the codicons "vm",
  "vm-connect", "vm-running", "vm-pending" and "vm-outline". Changed: scripts/build-icon-font.py
  combines, scales, moves and cuts these shapes. It also adds two shapes of its own: a filled screen,
  and the exclamation mark of the warning sign. The folder scripts/icons of the repository shows each
  result.
